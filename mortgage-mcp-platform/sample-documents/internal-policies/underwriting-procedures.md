# Underwriting Procedures — Conventional Delegated Workflow

## Scope
Conventional conforming mortgage loans delegated to Meridian underwriting through agency automated underwriting engines (desktop / loan product advisor frameworks) supplemented by overlays.

## Eligibility overlays (summary)
Borrowers must meet agency baseline PLUS Meridian overlays documented in underwriting matrix UW‑MX‑2026‑03.
- Bankruptcy / foreclosure seasoning additional months apply for borrower‑initiated short sales when loss severity exceeded model thresholds last 36 months.

## Verification hierarchy
Income: most recent 30‑day paystub + prior year W‑2 patterns or Form 4506‑c tax transcript validation when discrepancy flags trigger. Self‑employment requires two years Federal returns with year‑to‑date P&L and CPA licensure attestations when EBITDA adjustments exceed guideline caps.

Assets: sourcing of large deposits >50% monthly qualifying income flagged for narrative + paper trail; prohibited sources list maintained by compliance quarterly.
