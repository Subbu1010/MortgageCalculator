# Meridian Home Lending — Retail Loan Approval Workflow

**Policy ID:** MPL-POL-RTR-042  
**Audience:** Loan officers, processors, underwriting managers  
**Effective:** 2026-03-01

## Objective
Establish a repeatable, auditable path from initial application intake through issuance of underwriting decision and approval conditions for first-lien, owner-occupied residential mortgages.

## Stages

### 1. Intake & triage (Day 0)
- Verify application completeness via Loan Origination System (LOS): URLA 1003, intent to proceed, disclosures package.
- Obtain initial credit authorization. Run preliminary identity match against AML watchlists per **AML‑PROG‑019**.
- Route file by channel (retail, wholesale, delegated correspondent) using automated queue rules.

### 2. Pre-underwriting readiness
- Automated asset and income validations where supported; escalate when third-party validations fail thresholds.
- Order appraisal only after borrower consent and appraisal independence controls are satisfied under Reg Z / Appraiser Independence requirements for associated covered transactions where applicable.

### 3. Underwriting decision authority
Manual underwriting approvals at or above maximum agency delegations require managerial sign-off documented in underwriting notes within 72 hours.

## Documentation standards
Retain complete decision rationale, including models triggered, overrides, guideline references, compensating factors enumerated, and any fair lending anomaly notes when applicable under **Fair Lending Oversight Bulletin FL‑BRD‑06**.
